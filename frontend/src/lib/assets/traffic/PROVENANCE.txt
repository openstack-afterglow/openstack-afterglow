countries.geo.json is derived directly from that GeoJSON. Every polygon ring
and unrounded longitude/latitude vertex (including holes) is retained, with
the upstream ring orientation and antimeridian splits unchanged. Only
properties are reduced: ISO_A2_EH supplies country codes (absent codes remain
null, never guessed), NE_ID is the feature ID and ADMIN the upstream name.
The previous rounded equirectangular 720 x 360 SVG-path asset is removed.
Bklit UI's native ChoroplethChart/Mercator projects the bundled features;
country-to-site annotations use that same projection context. Anchors remain
the largest exterior polygon's bounding-box center in geographic degrees.
The adapter centers at [0, 20] with scale min(width / (2*pi), height / 4.8).
Mercator clips polar geography to its viewport, as expected for this projection.
No runtime third-party map requests. Small territories omitted by the 110m
source remain reachable in the observed country ranking. Boundaries and
logical access-flow annotations do not assert sovereignty or network routing.
