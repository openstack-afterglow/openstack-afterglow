## Why
The approved standalone loading artwork has an overly strong circular line. The user requests a softer, holy eclipse appearance with corona flares spilling around an occulting moon and light slowly falling away.

## What Changes
Replace the sharp ring with a cached diffuse corona, feathered dark disk, blurred rim and slowly descending/fading light particles. Retain the glyph rain, water reflection, status and motion lifecycle. Update standalone docs, screenshots and ZIP. No application route or dependency changes.
