## Why

Lumen's newer OpenAI routes expose model-specific image options and ticketed realtime modes. Afterglow must expose those features without inferring availability or treating estimates as charges.

## What Changes

- Add capability-driven Image Studio controls, preview formats and cost/usage feedback.
- Extend the existing browser-scoped ticket flow to live voice and translation.
- Render Lumen's effective catalog prices and reasoning choices without creating manual overrides.
- Preserve all permission, request ownership, credit settlement and provider-access boundaries.
